Privacy Policy for Turkish Drone 3D

Effective Date: November 24, 2026  |  Developer: VoraLabs  |  Package: com.turkishdrone3d.game

VoraLabs built Turkish Drone 3D as a free-to-play mobile game supported by optional rewarded video advertisements. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use our app.

1. Information We Collect

Turkish Drone 3D does not require user registration, accounts, or login, and VoraLabs does not directly collect or store your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or precise location. Game progress (such as currency, weapon levels, unlocked sorties, settings and language) is stored locally on your device only.

2. Third-Party Services & Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app integrates Google AdMob to display optional rewarded video advertisements (for example, to triple a reward or refill ammunition). Watching an ad is always the player's choice. Google AdMob may automatically collect and process certain device information, including:

  • Advertising ID (Google Advertising ID)
  • Device information, IP address (which can indicate approximate location), and diagnostic / crash logs
  • Ad interaction and performance data

Users in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom are asked for consent through Google's consent form before personalized ads are shown. All data transmitted by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit. You can learn more about how Google uses data from partner apps at Google Privacy & Terms, and you can reset or opt out of personalized ads at any time in your Android device settings (Settings → Google → Ads).

3. Children's Privacy

Turkish Drone 3D is intended for players aged 16 and older and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

Because we do not operate user accounts or remote player databases, clearing the app's storage or uninstalling Turkish Drone 3D removes all locally saved game progress from your device. For data processed by Google AdMob, see Google's privacy policy linked above.

5. Content Notice

All sides, flags and factions shown in the game are fictional. The game does not depict any real army, country or conflict, and it is not affiliated with or endorsed by any government or manufacturer.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
